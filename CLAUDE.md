# Grandoku

## Working agreement

Before making any change:

1. **Explain** what you intend to do and why, in plain terms (what files/behaviour change, and any trade-offs).
2. **Ask** for any details or clarifications you need, and confirm you've understood the request correctly.
3. **Wait for the go-ahead**, then build.

Don't start implementing, committing or deploying until the plan has been confirmed.
